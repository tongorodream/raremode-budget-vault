# Daily spending trend — Kelly Finance 0.3.3

**1M** on the Dashboard plots recorded expenses by day, within the budget period selected by the header. **Per day** is the default in 0.3.3: each point is that day's expense total, so the line can rise and fall. The **Cumulative** switch retains 0.3.2's accumulated reading. The chosen mode is saved as `chartTrendDailyMode` (`day`/`cumulative`), independently of the date range. For a calendar-month budget this is the selected month. A non-monthly pay cycle uses its actual boundaries and labels the control **1P**, rather than claiming seven days are a month.

In Per day mode a day without recorded expenses is zero; in Cumulative mode the balance carries forward. Only Cumulative has an opening zero point before the first day. Tooltips and keyboard focus show both readings. Future dates keep their positions on the axis but have no recorded expense/income points, so the unfinished month is not extended as a flat forecast.

The displayed line and its fill share a shape-preserving cubic Hermite interpolation. The actual day values remain unchanged: controls are slope-limited, local extrema have horizontal tangents, flat zero spans remain zero and intervals never overshoot their real endpoints. This is visual interpolation, not an average, forecast or reconstruction of intraday spending.

The cumulative budget limit is horizontal, when a spending budget exists. In Per day mode its reference is period budget / number of period days, explicitly labelled as an even daily pace rather than the whole period limit. Its above-pace colour is a comparison to that guide, not a claim that the period budget is exceeded. Income uses per-day or cumulative values consistently. The ledger's existing BUDGET exclusions still apply (excluded entries, non-budget/foreign accounts, earmarked outflows and transfers). Savings/investment allocations are excluded from this expense line. Refunds are not subtracted from gross recorded expenses.

Undated assume-spent provisions are disclosed separately; they are not invented as transactions on the first or last day. The sum of recorded daily expense totals (or the last Cumulative point) agrees with `budgetUsed(period).spent - budgetUsed(period).assumed`. Data is limited to recorded transactions, not a claim that a partial import covers all actual spending.

`chartTrendRange` defaults to `1m`. `chartTrendDefaultVersion: 1` marks the new default. On the first load of an older settings file, the in-memory range becomes `1m`; subsequent normal settings saves persist the marker. A later explicit range choice is retained across reloads. Loading does not perform an extra settings write. Multi-period ranges retain their previous calculations and historical-gap behaviour.
